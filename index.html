Stitch A family’s memories as an animated pop-up book. 2nd place at HackPrinceton. Video inside. 2026

Each family member adds photos and answers questions like “How did you meet Mom?” Stitch turns every answer into a page of a 3D pop-up book: the set rises off the paper, jointed paper puppets act out the memory, and the story is narrated in the teller’s own words.

Eight AI sub-agents built on LangChain deepagents storyboard, design and direct each scene. A compiler written in plain code then turns their screenplay into animation and rejects impossible actions, like handing over something a puppet isn’t holding.

Watch on YouTube

Built with two teammates. Next.js, three.js, FastAPI, LangGraph, MongoDB Atlas.

VPR Drone A drone that finds its position without GPS. Video inside. 2026

A drone that locates itself by sight. A visual place recognition model matches what the camera sees against a database of satellite images and returns the drone’s position. We tested KNN, MixVPR and DINO + VLAD retrieval.

The hardware is a Pixhawk 6C flight controller running ArduPilot, an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and a 12 MP camera.

Co-led a team of four. Python, ArduPilot, Jetson Orin Nano.

Atlas One calendar for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Canvas. 2026

A life manager for iOS and macOS built around a single calendar. It pulls in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar and Canvas, and organizes everything into spaces such as school, personal and business.

You type what’s on your mind in plain English, and Atlas turns it into tasks, files each one under the right part of your life, and helps place it on your timeline.

Built with a friend, in progress. SwiftUI, Supabase, EventKit, Google Calendar API.
