Stitch A family’s memories as an animated pop-up book. 2nd place at HackPrinceton. 2026

Family members add photos and answer questions like “How did you meet Mom?” Each answer becomes a page of a 3D pop-up book, acted out by paper puppets and narrated in the teller’s own words.

Eight AI agents storyboard and direct each scene, and a compiler turns their screenplay into animation.
